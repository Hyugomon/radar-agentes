What is an AI employee in Suparagent? It is a role-based AI agent with instructions, an engine and a workspace you configure for a kind of work. The role gives it a starting point; you still define the task and review the result. Can AI employees work together? Yes. You can bring a lead and specialists into a project room so tasks and handoffs stay together. Start with one clear job and add specialists when they help. What happens when I choose an employee template? It fills in the role and instructions in the create-agent form. It creates no agent, connects no account and grants no permission. You choose the engine, model, folder and approval mode, and can edit every field before creating it. Will an AI employee act without me? You choose the task, instructions and available environment. Suparagent supports manual and scheduled routines; try a routine manually and review its result before putting it on a schedule. What do I need to get started? For the hosted preview, use an email account, your Boat sandbox-provider key and an AI-provider account. Paid plans include the hosted computer when available. Does Suparagent include an AI subscription? You connect your own AI account. Provider eligibility, usage limits and charges still apply. Can I run it myself? The public core can run on your computer or server. Self-hosting means you manage installation, updates, access and backups. Which AI engines can I use? Claude and Codex sign-in have been exercised in the hosted preview. Other engine options depend on the installation and its available adapters.
