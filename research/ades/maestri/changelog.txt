Changelog · Maestri Changelog Maestri is frequently updated with new features, improvements, and fixes based on user feedback. Got an idea or something not working as expected? Reach out at bugs@themaestri.app . Or join our Discord . 0.61.0 November 23, 2026 New Questions in the chat: agents can now ask you a question with options to pick from. Click one and your answer lands in the Prompt Composer, quoting the question, ready for you to send. It works on other computers' terminals and in Maestri Remote too. Updated Inline code in notes now sits on a rounded background. More under-the-hood optimizations make panning, zooming and dragging on the canvas noticeably smoother, even on your busiest canvases. Stability and performance improvements. 0.60.0 November 18, 2026 New Spaces: organize your sidebar into spaces, each with its own icon and color, and switch between them in one click. A space can hold workspaces on this computer or on another computer you've connected over Maestri Wire, so everything you run lives in one sidebar. Remote Desktops: connect to Maestri on another computer on your network and work on its canvas as if it were your own, with its terminals, agents, notes, portals and file trees. Copy and paste between computers, files included. Agents across computers: rope agents together across computers, and ask, recruit or check a teammate on another computer by name, like "Reviewer @ Build Box". Open in New Window: open any workspace in its own window, or drag it out of the sidebar, and move it to another desktop or monitor. Works with workspaces on other computers too. Streaming Portals: control web and simulator portals remotely, from Maestri Remote or from another computer over Maestri Wire, Design mode included. Maestri Box: connect to Maestri Box, the headless edition of Maestri for Linux that you can self-host, and use it like any other computer in your sidebar. Manage its settings, license, roles, partituras and updates right from your Mac. Profiles: set your name, photo and computer name that your other computers and devices see, shown on the live cursors of whoever is working on a canvas with you. Updated Batuta now searches the workspaces, terminals and notes on every computer you're connected to. Maestri Wire settings are redesigned, with simpler pairing for the Remote app and for other computers, and every paired device in one place. Notifications from your connected computers show up on the Mac you're sitting at, and your phone stays quiet while you're at your desk. Workspaces on SSH, Docker or a custom environment can be set to always run there, so terminals, file trees and hooks never fall back to this Mac. Select several workspaces in the sidebar and drag them into a folder, or onto a space's icon to move them there. Ask a Maestro to start a teammate in another workspace by typing @ → Workspace… in the Prompt Composer. Portals now present themselves as your installed Safari, so more sites load without getting stuck on bot checks. 25 new icons for terminals, workspaces, bands and spaces. Copy and paste on the canvas from the Edit menu, and each paste lands beside the last one instead of on top of it. Stability and performance improvements. Point Releases 0.60.4 — November 22, 2026 Bug Fixes Fixed the shortcut shown in Edit Terminal for a terminal on another computer, which now shows the keys you press on this Mac. 0.60.3 — November 22, 2026 Dragging, panning and zooming are smoother on canvases with lots of ropes. Rope terminals across two workspaces on the same remote computer. Web portals keep loading and animating while your Mac's screen is locked, so agents and Maestri Remote see the page as it really is. In the chat, click anywhere on the working line to show or hide the terminal. Stability and performance improvements. 0.60.2 — November 19, 2026 Text typed into a portal from Maestri Remote or by an agent now reaches sign-in forms inside embedded frames, like App Store Connect's.
