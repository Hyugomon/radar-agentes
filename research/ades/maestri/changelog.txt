Changelog · Maestri Changelog Maestri is frequently updated with new features, improvements, and fixes based on user feedback. Got an idea or something not working as expected? Reach out at bugs@themaestri.app . Or join our Discord . 0.59.0 November 3, 2026 New Computer Use: agents can read and use other apps on your computer in the background, without moving your pointer or taking your keyboard. They ask before using each app, and you can answer on your computer or from Maestri Remote. An agent can also ask to control the whole computer with your pointer and keyboard while you're away. Turn it on in Settings → Agents → Control. Updated Links in chat and notes use the surrounding text color with an underline, so they're easier to read. Bug Fixes Fixed jumping to a terminal or workspace with 1–9 on keyboard layouts whose number row doesn't type digits. Fixed F2 opening rename instead of reaching the program in a focused terminal. Fixed clicking below a chat's last message deselecting the terminal. 0.59.3 — November 6, 2026 Touch support on the canvas, optimized for the new MacBook Pro with PROLED display. Chat terminals have a Clear Chat History button in their toolbar. Computer Use can pick items from pop-up menus in other apps. You can turn off haptic feedback when moving elements on the canvas, in Settings → Canvas. With a long message in the composer, you can scroll up to see the chat history.
