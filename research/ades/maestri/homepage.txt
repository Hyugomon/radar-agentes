Maestri · The infinite canvas for orchestrating AI agents Now you're conducting. Meet Maestri, the first infinite canvas made for orchestrating agents. Every agent gets a role, they talk to each other directly, and the whole operation stays in front of you. The industry wants you to believe the answer is a chatbot with an inbox. It makes sense for them: a queue of finished tasks is cheap to build and looks like progress. Run several agents and the project takes on a state none of them can see. Which path each one took, what was already ruled out, where two are about to collide. That map lives in your head, and you rebuild it from scratch every time you come back. Maestri keeps that map on the canvas. You come back, look, and already know where everything stands. Maestri by @evertjr What's on the canvas Everything you need, no distractions. Teamwork. Connect terminals together and let agents prompt each other directly. One agent can delegate tasks, ask questions, or hand off work without you lifting a finger. Give each agent a role. Create reusable roles with custom instructions and assign them to agents. One leads, one codes, one reviews, one tests. Each agent knows exactly what to do. Unslopifier Bug Whisperer Vibe Coder Ship It Refactor Gremlin Docs Goblin Git Wizard Perf Nerd New Shell we talk? Every terminal gets a chat face. Ask in a message, get an answer in formatted text, with diagrams, tables and files you can drag onto the canvas. Seven subjects with the same agent, and you never lose the thread. What if your terminal had no edges? Pan freely. Zoom in on the agent that needs attention. Zoom out to see the full picture. Group related work spatially, the way your brain already thinks about projects. MaestriCanvas engine, built from scratch Spawn agents anywhere. Drop a new terminal wherever you need it. Each one runs its own agent: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or just a plain shell. They all stay on screen at the same time, exactly where you put them. Give your agents a browser. And a device. Portals are embedded windows on your canvas. A website, an iOS Simulator, an Android emulator, or a real phone plugged into your Mac. Connect one to an agent and it clicks, types, taps, and swipes, reading the app's real element tree instead of guessing at pixels. Get to the prompt. A rich composer floats over any terminal. Type @ to point the agent at a connected terminal, note, or portal, paste screenshots straight into the text, and every terminal keeps its own draft until you send. Notes, written by your agents. Connect a terminal to a sticky note and let your agent write directly to it. Progress updates, summaries, decisions, captured automatically while you focus on what matters. One canvas per context. Separate canvases for separate projects. Jump between terminals and workspaces with tmux-style shortcuts that feel instant. Each workspace remembers exactly where you left off. Draw your thinking. Sketch architecture diagrams, draw arrows between components, map out flows, right on the canvas, next to the agents doing the work. Hand-drawn shapes that feel natural and fast. Bands Too many agents to follow? Make them a band. Gather terminals in one place and switch between them like chats. Every member stays a real terminal with its own session and role. The list shows who's working and who needs you, Maestros sit on top, and a single connection links the whole band to a note or a portal. Ask your Maestro and it sorts the team into bands on its own. Floors Context switch without losing context. Need to fix a bug on another branch? Review a PR? Try something risky? Create a floor. It's an instant, isolated copy of your entire workspace. New branch, new terminals, zero interference with your current work. When you're done, land it back. Or don't. APFS copy-on-write. Instant clone. Nearly zero disk space. Computer Use Not everything lives in a terminal. Your agents can use your apps. They work in other apps in the background, without moving your pointer or taking your keyboard. Each agent asks before it uses an app, and you can answer on your computer or from Maestri Remote. Every agent has a cursor with its name, several can work at once, and when only your real pointer and keyboard will do, an agent can ask to take over while you're away. Ombro Always on your shoulder. Never in your way. Ombro is an on-device AI companion that keeps an eye on your agents while you do other things. When an agent finishes a task, it summarizes what happened and suggests what to do next, in a floating window that lives outside the app so you never miss a beat. Powered by Apple Foundation Models, it runs entirely on your Mac. Answers come back at local speed and stay on the machine. A quiet intelligence on your shoulder. 100% on-device Apple Foundation Models Ombro requires macOS 26 or later. Built for Mac 100% native. Zero compromise. Swift From the UI to the terminal emulator, everything is Swift and SwiftUI. Pixel-perfect TUI rendering at every zoom level. Native code all the way down. AppKit Real macOS interface with the new Liquid Glass design. System conventions, dark mode, and accessibility built in. Buttery smooth GPU-accelerated canvas powered by Metal. Pan, zoom, and draw without a single dropped frame. Minimal RAM footprint so your agents get the resources, not the app. Apple Intelligence Ombro runs on Foundation Models. Private, fast, completely on-device. Privacy Your work stays yours. Period. Maestri was built with one rule: your data never leaves your Mac. Zero telemetry The app never phones home. How you use Maestri is nobody's business, including ours. Fully local Your workspaces, notes, and canvas live on your machine. Configuration is plain JSON, notes are Markdown files. Nothing is uploaded. No account required Download it and start working. Maestri never asks who you are. On-device AI Ombro processes everything on your Mac. Your agent summaries never leave your device. Coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, and others have their own privacy policies and may send data to their respective providers. Maestri does not control or intercept that traffic. A conductor doesn't play every instrument, but without them, there is no symphony. AI agents are your orchestra. The music is still yours.
