Almost anything in bb can be changed in a single prompt. Ask for your Linear issues and they appear: a panel in your sidebar, a bb linear command, and a skill that teaches every agent to use it. Many of bb’s own features are built with the same tools you have. The GitHub integration, agent memory, scheduled jobs, and even remote access are all plugins. Nothing is stopping you from building your ideal workbench.
