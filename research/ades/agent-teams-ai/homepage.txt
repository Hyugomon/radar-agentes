Agent Teams Build mixed teams with Claude, Codex, Gemini, and OpenCode-connected providers. Give agents distinct roles, let them work autonomously in parallel, and coordinate across team boundaries. Kanban Board Agents manage the cards themselves: they pick up tasks, update statuses, and move work across the board. You do not have to touch the Kanban manually, but you stay in control. Changes Viewer Review task and teammate changes in one Cursor-style workspace. Navigate files and full diffs, accept or reject whole files or individual hunks, edit when needed, undo decisions, and send feedback. Token Analytics & Budgets See where tokens and estimated costs go across teams, agents, tasks, projects, models, and runs. Spot expensive work, track trends and forecasts, set monthly token or cost budgets, and get alerts at 80% and 100%. Organizations & Global Overview Organize teams into departments, squads, or any nested structure. See every organization on one live map with team and agent statuses, task progress, dependencies, delegation, and cross-team communication. Terminal Run commands in a visual terminal workspace with team and local shells, persistent history, autocomplete, and terminal settings. Cross-Team Communication Agents message each other within and across teams. Direct messaging, task comments, and quick actions. Solo Mode Start with a single agent that self-manages tasks. Expand to a full team whenever you need more power.
