Conductor 0.93.0: Conductor is joining Meta https://www.conductor.build/changelog/0.93.0-conductor-is-joining-meta https://www.conductor.build/changelog/0.93.0-conductor-is-joining-meta Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:00:00 GMT The Conductor mascot wearing a Conductor cap Conductor is joining Meta. Thank you for coming along for the ride! Read the announcement. We'll be winding down Conductor over the next weeks and months: Conductor Cloud goes read-only on November 13 and shuts down on November 20. Local Conductor keeps getting new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027. To move a cloud workspace and its chats to your Mac, right-click it and choose Transfer to local. To download all your data, go to Settings → Account → Export data. Update: Conductor isn't shutting down. Conductor is back. If you need anything, email support@conductor.build. Improvements Add your own completion sounds in Settings → General. Pick a sound file, name it, and choose it like any other sound. Archived cloud workspaces show their PRs, last known changes, and file tree in the right panel. Added GPT-6.1 Terra to the Codex model picker. Fixes Fixed the Claude account usage display when Claude Code reports newer limit data. Fixed closed chats in chat history showing a recent time when nothing had happened in them. Messages that agents send to other agents now show a "Send message" label with the recipient and contents. The rename workspace dialog now prefills the name shown in the sidebar instead of the worktree name. Long PR titles no longer hide the PR number in the PR view. Expanded Mermaid diagrams fill the window instead of opening in a narrow popup. Conductor 0.92.0: Remote control https://www.conductor.build/changelog/0.92.0-remote-control https://www.conductor.build/changelog/0.92.0-remote-control Fri, 23 Oct 2026 00:00:00 GMT The QR code for pairing the Conductor iPhone app with a Mac Control your Mac from your iPhone. With remote control, you can browse and create workspaces, read chats, send messages, pick models, and stop agents in your Mac's local workspaces from the Conductor iOS app. Turn on Remote control in Settings → Experimental, then scan the QR code with the iOS app. Learn how to set it up. Remote control now stays connected while the Conductor window is hidden or minimized, shows the full history of older chats, and transfers much less data. It also keeps your Mac awake while your iPhone is connected and the Mac is plugged in. You can turn this off in Settings → Remote control.
