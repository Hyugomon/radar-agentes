In English and Chinese. 74 秒，看 nanoMuse 在手机、电脑和网页上做什么。有英文和中文两版。 The file: English · 中文 文件： English · 中文 What it is 这是什么 nanoMuse is one agent on your Android phone, iPhone and iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux and the web. The devices share one account and show the same conversations: say it on the phone, it runs on your PC, and the approval comes back to the device in your hand. nanoMuse 是同一个智能体，在你的 Android 手机、iPhone 和 iPad、Mac、Windows、Linux 和网页上。几台设备共用一个账号，看到同样的对话：在手机上说一句，它在你的电脑上执行，要确认的事回到你手里的这台设备上。 It does things instead of answering questions: a shell, a browser, MCP servers and skills, and with Hands on, the apps on your phone and the windows on your computer through their screens. The agent never runs a tool itself: every call goes through the Sentinel, which stops to ask before deleting, sending or paying. 它做事，而不只是回答：有 shell、浏览器、MCP 服务器和技能；打开「手」之后，还能隔着屏幕操作手机上的应用和电脑上的窗口。智能体自己从不执行工具：每一次调用都经过 Sentinel，删除、发送、付款之前它会先停下来问你。 What it is, what it knows about you and when it wakes are Markdown files you can read and edit. 它是谁、它知道你的什么、它什么时候醒来，都是你能读也能改的 Markdown 文件。 Free, open source, non-profit. Let's build it together. The phone app, the desktop app, the web console and the relay that joins them are one repository under GPL-3.0-or-later. Sign in to the community relay and you get a free allowance of model use and your devices in sync; with a key of your own the runtime needs no account at all, and the same relay runs on a server of yours. 免费、开源、非营利。一起把它做好。 手机应用、桌面应用、网页控制台和把它们连起来的中继在同一个仓库里，GPL-3.0-or-later。登录社区中继，有一份免费的模型额度，设备之间互相同步；用自己的 key，运行时完全不需要账号；同一个中继也可以跑在你自己的服务器上。 nanoMuse keeps evolving nanoMuse 在持续进化 A personal agent should not live only in someone else's cloud, so nanoMuse is the open-source, low-cost alternative: on your own devices, with a model you choose and a relay you can host yourself. We want to build it with developers and users everywhere, so that each person truly owns a personal agent. Open an issue or send a pull request; either one helps nanoMuse get better. 个人智能体不该只长在别人的云里，nanoMuse 想做一个开源、低成本的平替：装在自己的设备上，模型自己选，中继也能自己架。我们希望和全球的开发者、使用者一起，让每个人真正拥有自己的个人智能体。提个 issue 或者发个 PR 都是在帮 nanoMuse 变得更好。 Open an issue 提个 issue Send a pull request 发个 PR Download 下载 Version 1.0.9 “Tiller”, 2026-10-29 · release notes and checksums 版本 1.0.9「Tiller」，2026-10-29 · 发布说明与校验值 Android 8.0+, arm64 Android 8.0 以上，arm64 APK iPhone, iPad TestFlight · a beta that passed Apple's beta review; the link installs it, and a new version reaches it once its own review has passed · iPhone and iPad in the docs 测试版，已通过 Apple 的 beta 审核；从这个链接安装，新版本在它自己的审核通过后到达 · 文档里的 iPhone 和 iPad 一页 Windows 10+ x64 · .exe macOS 12+ Apple Silicon · .dmg · .zip Intel · .dmg · .zip Linux x64 AppImage · .deb · .tar.gz Browser 浏览器 demo.nanomuse.dev · the demo at the top of this page, on its own: a nanoMuse on a simulated phone, after a sign-in 页首的演示，单独一页：一台模拟手机上的 nanoMuse，登录后体验 Docker ghcr.io/nano-muse/nanomuse:1.0.9 · the runtime for the web app on a computer of yours; a relay of your own: self-hosting 在你自己的电脑上跑网页版的运行时；自己的中继见 自己部署 Every version 全部版本 github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/releases Every Android build is signed with the same key and installs over the last.
