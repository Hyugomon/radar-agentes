What do I need? A Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) or a Windows 11 PC (x64), and a Claude plan or an Anthropic API key. Setup gets Claude Code ready for you: it installs it if needed, then signs you in. Each team member is a Claude Code agent with its own folder and memory. A Claude Max plan keeps the office running all day; on smaller plans the office takes a break when you reach your usage limit: scheduled jobs still go out on time and wait in the inbox, and the team picks up by itself when it resets. With an API key you pay Anthropic for what the team uses, and connectors such as QuickBooks come with a Claude account. If your Anthropic account runs out of credit, your Claude sign in expires or your API key stops working, the office stops and says why, with Add credit, Sign in or Change key, and scheduled jobs wait in the inbox. Linux is to follow. Can an agent spend money or email customers on its own? Money, never: no agent pays a bill, moves money or promises a refund or discount without your approval, and none deletes an email or posts in public. Email, only if you let it: a new mailbox starts on Send on approval, so each email waits on Needs you first, and the team member sends exactly what you approved, unless you tell them to send that kind without asking, which you can revoke on their Access tab. Draft only keeps replies in Drafts for you. Can send lets one send on its own. If the connection drops mid-send, an email that may have gone out is held, never sent twice. Where does my data go? Work, memory, files and mailbox passwords are kept on your computer (passwords encrypted with a key your system keeps, in the Keychain on a Mac). What an agent reads for a job goes to Claude through your own plan or API key. When your team needs the web, they're told to ask Pete, the Research team member, who is off until you turn them on: every question the app passes to Pete is checked against what you keep private first, and the app gives Pete questions only, never your files. The app sends no usage data, only failure reports with no names, messages or files. Which mailboxes work? Gmail, Google Workspace, iCloud, Yahoo, Zoho or any IMAP mailbox, each watched by one team member. If you choose Only send email, a teammate can also send from an address another teammate watches, such as yours, and isn't given that inbox to read. Outlook and Microsoft 365 aren't supported yet. Is there a paid version? No. No paid version, no trial. It's MIT licensed, and the code is on GitHub. Stop doing every job yourself.
