If yours is not in there, the founders are right below. General How it works Features & usage Plans & billing Why is a Coworker better than Claude or ChatGPT at the same task? Because a chatbot stops at the answer. It writes the email, and you still open the tab, copy the text, log into the old portal and send it. A Coworker has its own computer and your apps, so it does those steps itself, and it starts on its own at 7am, without you opening anything. I do not know what to hand one first. Where do I start? Take the thing you redo every week and dread doing: the invoice chase, the inbox triage, the Monday report. The examples on this page are the ones people hand over first. Can I start from something ready-made? Yes. The library has Coworkers already built for a trade: agencies, e-commerce, SaaS, sales, recruiting and more. Install one, point it at your own apps, and change the parts that are not how you work. Can someone set my Coworkers up for me? Yes. Rerun experts build and install Coworkers for a living, and you can browse them and hire one. The two of us who build Rerun also answer in the Discord, for free. Is my data safe? Your workspace has its own private machine, walled off from every other customer, and your data stays in it. We do not train models on your content, and the model providers we call are contractually barred from it too. What do I actually have to set up? Three things, once. Connect the apps your business already uses, hire a Coworker and tell it its job, then say when it should run. It takes it from there. How long before my first Coworker is working? A few minutes. Pick a ready-made Coworker or describe the job in your own words, connect a tool or two, and it starts. Where do my Coworkers actually run? On their own machine in the cloud, in Europe, one per workspace and isolated from everyone else. It is always on, and its files, its memory and its databases stay inside it. Does it keep running when my laptop is closed? Yes. Your Coworkers live on their own machine in the cloud, which never sleeps. Close your laptop, go to bed: the 7am run still happens, and the result is there when you come back. Does a Coworker remember my business between runs? Yes, and that is most of its value. It keeps its own files, its own notes and what it has learned about your offer, your market and your clients, so it knows Acme pays at 60 days without you saying it twice. Monday, a chatbot starts from zero; your Coworker opens the file it has been filling for weeks. What if a Coworker does something I did not want? It stops and asks first. Before anything consequential, like spending money, emailing a client or deleting something, it pauses and waits for your yes. And every run is on the board, so nothing happens that you cannot see afterwards. What tools can my Coworkers use? Stripe, HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Google Drive and 3,000+ more, plus any MCP server, and your Coworker wires up the ones its job needs by itself. It even uses the tools that have no connector: it opens them on its own computer, the way you would. Can Coworkers work together? Yes. They hand work to each other: one finds the leads, the next writes to them, a third files the result. You ask for it once, and the chain runs without you in the middle. Can I watch them work, live? Yes, that is the whole idea. Every Coworker is on one board, updated in real time while it works, with the full history of every run when you want the detail. Can I take over in the middle of a run? Yes. Open its screen and take the mouse: do the login yourself, fix the field it got wrong, then hand it back. It carries on from that page. How do I know when something goes wrong? It tells you. A failed run, a question, an approval waiting, an app that lost its connection: each one raises the Coworker on the board and reaches you by email or as a notification, on whichever of them you want to be told about. What if a Coworker gets stuck while I am away? It waits, and anyone on your team can answer it. It picks up exactly where it stopped, seconds later. You can also leave it rules to answer by itself, so it only waits on the calls you want to make yourself. Can I use Rerun from my phone? Yes. Rerun runs in your phone browser and installs to your home screen like an app. The board, the conversation and the approvals are all built for a small screen, so you can unblock a Coworker from a queue. How does billing work? Do I pay per person? Never per person. $29 a month gets you unlimited Coworkers, their private machine and as many teammates as you want in the workspace, at no extra cost. The only thing that grows with you is your Rerun credit. That credit is not only for the models we host: it also pays for the requests your Coworkers make to the 3,000+ tools. Is a Claude or ChatGPT subscription required? No. Every plan comes with credit on the models we host, so your Coworkers work on day one with nothing to connect. Plugging in your own Claude or ChatGPT plan is there for when you already pay for one: those runs then cost you nothing on our side, and we never count them. Which AI models can I use? Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and more, and you choose per Coworker: the fast cheap model for sorting an inbox, the strongest one for the job that has to be right. What do the emails, phone numbers and other data cost? They come out of your Rerun credit, and an email or a phone number is only billed when it is found. Before a search, your Coworker tells you what it will cost and waits for your yes. There is no data provider to subscribe to. Clément Janssens Co-founder Théo Louro Co-founder Still have a question?
