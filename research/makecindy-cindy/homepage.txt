Cindy brings together the strengths of different harnesses, shaped by our own product vision and attention to detail: intuitive interactions, a cohesive experience across devices, and fluid remote access. Keep your familiar subscriptions and work across your phone and multiple computers. Cindy is open source and grows with contributions from the community. You can contribute improvements, or shape her through memory, skills, plugins and source code to make Cindy your own. How is a harness different from a model? Claude Code, Codex and Pi are agent harnesses that organize execution. Models do the reasoning and generation. Cindy offers both choices; available combinations depend on the provider, authorization and model capabilities. Can my phone keep tasks running when my computer is off? Remote tasks execute on your computer, which needs to be online with remote access enabled. Mobile can interact with reachable computers; it does not automatically move offline work into the cloud. Is Cindy free? Can I use an existing subscription? The client is free. Authorize a supported subscription or connect your own API key. Your provider's usage fees and limits still apply. Cindy's managed model service is an optional, separately billed choice. Can I use local models? Use Ollama, llama.cpp or local services such as LM Studio. Local models remain in beta; results depend on the model, hardware and runtime. Where do my files, code and conversations go? Local-first does not mean no data ever leaves your device. Model inference sends necessary conversation and file content to your selected service. Companion cloud sync, remote devices and external plugins also transfer relevant data. Choose services, permissions and sync settings to fit your needs, and read the privacy policy. Are skills and plugins available now? Yes. Browse, install and update skills and plugins, or create and share your own. Third-party services may still require their own accounts or subscriptions. How do I apply for Team or Enterprise? Join the waitlist with your email. We will contact you when access opens. Team focuses on shared setup and governance; Enterprise adds self-hosting and support options.
